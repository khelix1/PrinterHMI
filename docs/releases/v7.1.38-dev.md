# v7.1.38-dev — Quiet connection recovery

Quiet continues to show confirmed connection-loss warnings. Once a printer
recovers, its offline warning clears without a new reconnected/online card in
Quiet. Both transitions remain in Event History. Standard and Detailed retain
loss and recovery cards. This applies to active and inactive printers.

Connection notices now have an explicit internal category. Switching to Quiet
removes queued connection-recovery cards but retains connection-loss warnings,
printer faults, paused-print warnings and other essential recovery instructions.
The preference descriptions match the policy. Existing debounce, profile/endpoint
baselines, health confirmation and notice ownership remain. There is no transport
or stale-data timing change, and no new persisted field or backup migration.

Real-owner notification tests cover repeated loss/recovery at all levels, history
records, pending-card filtering, active/inactive identity, faults, pause, queue
priority, bootstrap, preferences, lifecycle and layout across themes/text sizes.
Existing Settings, backup preference and connection-race checks also run. The
full firmware toolchain is unavailable here; build with
`./tools/build_idf6_hosted3.sh`, then check a confirmed loss/recovery in Quiet and
Standard and confirm that warnings/faults remain visible.

Apply incrementally over v7.1.37-dev on `chore/stale-code-cleanup-main`. The updater
has source guards, backups, rollback and recovery. It performs no commit, push,
merge, build, flash or printer action.
